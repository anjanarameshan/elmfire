How to decide

Survived: same roof shape, colour and position in both images. Moss, a car or new trees don't matter.

Damaged: the building is still there but part of it is missing or repaired, such as a lost deck or a patched roof section.

Destroyed: 2018 shows an empty lot, a bare slab, or a different new building. A house that was rebuilt counts as destroyed.

 

Not built yet: there was no building inside the yellow outline in 2016, only trees or bare ground. These are left out of the validation.

Can't tell: trees or shadow hide the roof, or both images are too blurry.

Keys: 1–5 answer, ← / → move, G opens Google Earth.

When to use Google Earth

Use it when the 2018 roof looks similar but not quite the same, since a rebuilt house can look like the old one. In Google Earth, click the clock icon and choose Dec 2, 2016 (four days after the fire). In that false-colour image, a burned house shows as dark grey or black rubble and green vegetation shows red or pink.

Keep one tab on Dec 2, 2016; the date can reset when a link opens.
